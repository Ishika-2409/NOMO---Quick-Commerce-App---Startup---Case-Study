1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
NOMO is a hyperlocal quick-commerce platform focused on underserved Tier-2 and Tier-3 Indian cities.
Rather than investing in dark stores, NOMO enables existing neighborhood retailers to fulfill online demand efficiently.
This strategy minimizes operational costs, accelerates market expansion, and strengthens local businesses while delivering 
a fast and convenient customer experience.


2. STRATEGIC OBJECTIVE
- Become the preferred quick-commerce platform in Tier-2 cities.
- Enable digital transformation for local merchants.
- Assist Local Merchant/stores with standardizing stores and manage logistics
- Achieve sustainable unit economics without dark stores.


3. MARKET OPPORTUNITY 
## Growth of Indian quick commerce
1. Growth of Quick Commerce (2020–2025)
Indias quick-commerce market moved from a niche concept in 2020 to a multi-billion-dollar industry by 2025, driven by changing consumer behavior,
smartphone penetration, digital payments, and investments by Blinkit, Zepto, and Swiggy Instamart.



## Tier-2 population without Quick-commerce service (Zepto, Instamart, Blinkit)
2. Expansion Beyond Metro Cities 
Quick-commerce is no longer restricted to metro cities. Leading players are increasingly targeting Tier-2 markets, 
validating demand beyond Indias largest urban centers.

## Doom of Local retail market
5. Local Retail Challenge
Local retailers face increasing competitive pressure in cities where quick-commerce platforms expand rapidly.
Many independent stores have experienced reduced walk-in traffic and greater price competition,
prompting them to adopt omnichannel strategies or partner with digital platforms. 
Physical retail, however, continues to dominate India's overall retail market.
